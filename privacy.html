Privacy Policy

Sleep Factory All Night · Effective 28 October 2026

Who we are

"Sleep Factory" is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Sleep Factory All Night to publish its own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app does

The app generates original sleep music videos on a private server and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. To do this, the owner signs in once with Google and grants permission to upload and manage videos on that channel.

What data is accessed

  • A Google OAuth access token and refresh token for the owner's account, used only to upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists on the owner's channel.
  • No personal data of viewers or any other person is collected, requested or processed.

How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the account that runs the app. It is never sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and it is not used for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than publishing the owner's own videos.

Google API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all uploads, and the stored token becomes useless.
